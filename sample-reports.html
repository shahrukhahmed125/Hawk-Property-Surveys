Sample: RICS Level 3 Building Survey Report

Property: 4-Bedroom Victorian Townhouse, London W8 Condition Rating 3

File Ref: HPS-L3-2026-0881 | Format: PDF (64 Pages) | Inspecting Surveyor: M. R. (MRICS)

1.0 Chimney Stacks & High-Level Masonry (Rating 3)

The main masonry chimney stack to the left party wall shows extensive frost spalling to external brickwork courses. The top cement flaunching has fractured into loose segments, creating a potential falling hazard during severe gales. Lead step and cover flashings have detached from mortar chases.

2.0 Sub-Floor Timbers & Ventilation (Rating 2)

Ground floor suspended timbers show elevated moisture content (22.4% WME) due to two external terracotta air bricks being obstructed by a raised exterior patio. Re-establishing sub-floor airflow is required to avert wet rot.

Estimated Priority Remedial Works: £3,800 – £5,200 Negotiation Leverage: High
