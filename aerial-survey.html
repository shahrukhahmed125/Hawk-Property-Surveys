High-Altitude Building Inspection

Aerial & Drone Roof Surveys.

Safe, non-invasive high-resolution 4K optical inspection of otherwise inaccessible roofs, chimney stacks, parapets, and multi-storey façades without costly scaffolding.

Safety & Coverage

See Every Tile, Ridge and Flashing Clearly.

In standard ground-level property inspections, high-pitched roofs, rear valleys, and towering chimney stacks cannot be safely observed with the naked eye or ladders. Defective valleys and slipped slates frequently go unnoticed until water pours into ceiling joists.

Hawk Property Surveys deploys CAA-certified commercial unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) equipped with stabilized 4K optical zoom cameras. We inspect hidden roof pitches, lead secret gutters, copings, and high-level architectural details in minutes, saving clients thousands in unnecessary scaffolding costs.

What We Inspect via Aerial Drone

  • Chimney Stacks & Pots: Mortar flaunching integrity, structural lean, brickwork spalling, and lead soaker flashings.
  • Slate & Tile Coverings: Displaced ridge tiles, nail fatigue (slate slip), cracked tiles, and moss accumulation.
  • Parapets & Valley Gutters: Split lead linings, defective box gutters, blocked rainwater outlets, and coping stones.
  • Multi-Storey Façades: External cladding joints, high-level render fractures, and window seal condition.
