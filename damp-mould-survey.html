Scientific Methodology

Why Independent Damp Diagnosis Matters.

Over 80% of properties diagnosed with "rising damp" by commercial damp-proofing contractors are actually suffering from condensation, cold bridging, high ground levels, or defective rainwater goods.

Contractors offering "free damp surveys" make their profit by selling invasive chemical injection damp-proof courses (DPCs) and destructive plaster stripping. Hawk Property Surveys operates with zero commercial ties to remedial firms. Our PCA-qualified building pathologists use calibrated instrumentation to identify the exact mechanism of moisture ingress.

The Four Moisture Mechanisms We Diagnose

  • 1. Condensation & Black Mould: Interstitial cold spots, inadequate extraction rates, air tightness imbalances, and relative humidity exceeding 70%.
  • 2. Penetrating Damp: Defective mortar pointing, porous masonry, cracked render, leaking gutters, hopper head overflow, or roof valley leaks.
  • 3. Rising Damp: Capillary draw of ground moisture through permeable masonry, confirmed through salt analysis (nitrates/chlorides) and moisture gradient mapping.
  • 4. Lateral Moisture & Ground Bridging: External paths, patios, or flowerbeds built above the internal damp-proof course bridging the masonry barrier.

Our Diagnostic Toolset

We employ Protimeter digital moisture meters (Wood Moisture Equivalent WME and non-invasive radio frequency search modes), calibrated thermo-hygrometers for surface dew-point calculation, FLIR infrared thermal imaging, and chemical on-site salt analysis.
