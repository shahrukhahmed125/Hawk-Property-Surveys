Independent Property Surveyors Committed to Unbiased Clarity.

With over 25 years of combined UK surveying experience, we deliver rigorous building pathology, RICS-compliant condition surveys, and forensic defect analysis with complete independence from remedial contractors.

Our Story

Built on Technical Precision and Uncompromising Independence.

Headquartered at 135 Spring Grove Crescent, Hounslow (London TW3 4DA), Hawk Property Surveys was founded to solve a pervasive problem in the property industry: conflicted advice.

Too often, prospective homebuyers and property owners turn to damp-proofing firms or remedial contractors offering "free surveys"—only to receive alarmist reports recommending thousands of pounds of invasive, unnecessary chemical injection or destructive timber replacement.

Hawk Property Surveys was established on a single foundational principle: true independence. We do not sell remedial treatments, we do not undertake building work, and we take no referral fees from contractors. When you instruct Hawk, you are hiring an impartial technical advocate whose sole objective is truthful building pathology.

25+ Years of Evolution

A Quarter-Century of Building Pathology.

From historic Georgian and Victorian solid brick terraces to modern steel-framed residential towers, our team has accumulated deep diagnostic experience across all British architectural eras.

Period Building Specialists

Extensive expertise in traditional breathable construction: lime mortars, solid masonry, lath and plaster, timber framing, and suspended timber ground floors.

Cutting-Edge Instrumentation

We invest continuously in precision instrumentation: calibrated radio-frequency hygrometers, FLIR thermal imaging cameras, endoscopes, and ultrasonic leak detectors.

Multi-Disciplinary Accreditation

Our surveyors are accredited across RICS, Property Care Association (PCA), and the Faculty of Party Wall Surveyors, bridging general surveying with forensic moisture science.

Governance & Credentials

Regulated by the UK's Leading Surveying Bodies.

Our practices adhere strictly to the professional codes of conduct, continuing professional development (CPD), and comprehensive professional indemnity insurance mandated by top institutions.

RICS Regulated

Regulated by the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors. We operate in strict compliance with the RICS Home Survey Standard, guaranteeing methodical rigor and professional indemnity protection.

PCA Certified Remedial Surveyors

Accredited by the Property Care Association (PCA). Certified in damp diagnostics (CRDS), timber infestation analysis, and structural waterproofing to British Standard BS 8102.

Faculty of Party Wall Surveyors

Corporate members of the Faculty of Party Wall Surveyors (FPWS), appointed to act as Agreed Surveyor or Building Owner Surveyor under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996.

Core Principles

The Four Pillars of Our Surveying Practice.

1. Total Independence

We refuse all contractor kickbacks and commercial affiliate relationships. Our reports are 100% impartial and evidence-led.

2. Scientific Rigor

We don't guess based on surface appearance. We measure moisture gradients, thermal profiles, and environmental equilibrium.

3. Actionable Clarity

No dense technical jargon that obscures reality. We tell you exactly what is wrong, how urgent it is, and what it costs to fix.

4. Ongoing Surveyor Access

Our relationship doesn't end when the PDF report is sent. Your inspecting surveyor is available by phone to discuss every point.

5. Rapid Turnaround

Delivered typically within 3 to 5 business days, protecting your purchase momentum and legal transaction schedules.

6. Guaranteed Fixed Quotes

Transparent, upfront quotes. What we quote is what you pay—no surprise mileage or ancillary fees.
