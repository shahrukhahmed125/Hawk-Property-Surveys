About Hawk Property Surveys

Understanding Your Property Starts With The Right Expertise.

At Hawk Property Surveys, we combine meticulous building pathology with independent advice to ensure property purchasers, owners, and managers receive transparent clarity on building defects, structural integrity, and remedial priorities.

Sample Report: RICS Level 3 Home Survey

Subject Property: 4-Storey Victorian Terraced Townhouse, London Urgent Action Required

Inspection Ref: HPS-2026-L3-0881 | Inspecting Surveyor: M. R. (MRICS, MCIOB)

Executive Summary & Critical Findings

The main roof pitch shows evidence of progressive tile slippage and deteriorated battens on the rear slope. Valley gutters show fractured lead flashing allowing water ingress into the ceiling void of the attic bedroom.

Sub-floor ventilation to the front ground floor reception is obstructed by subsequent paving, leading to elevated moisture content (24.8% WME) in perimeter wall plates and joist ends. Remedial air bricks and timber preservative treatment are advised.

Sample Report: Damp & Timber Pathology Audit

Subject Property: Ground Floor Edwardian Conversion Flat, Surrey Remedial Action Advised

Inspection Ref: HPS-2026-DT-1142 | Inspecting Surveyor: S. K. (CSRT, PCA)

Pathology Analysis: Condensation vs Rising Damp

A chemical injection damp proof course had been quoted by a commercial contractor at £8,500. Our calibrated hygrometry and salt analysis demonstrated zero nitrate/chloride concentrations associated with rising groundwater.

The damp was solely caused by interstitial cold bridging and defective mechanical bathroom extraction. Correcting extraction ducting and applying thermal dry-lining resolved the condition at a total cost of £820.

Sample Report: FLIR Thermographic Audit

Subject Property: Detached Executive Home, Middlesex Satisfactory Performance

Inspection Ref: HPS-2026-IR-0490 | Inspecting Surveyor: D. B. (ITC Level 2 Thermographer)

Thermal Envelope & Insulation Assessment

Calibrated infrared imaging identified a 4.2°C temperature drop along the junction of the north-facing ceiling and external wall, indicative of displaced mineral wool insulation above the eaves.

Underfloor heating loop mapping successfully identified a localized minor pressure drop without invasive tile removal, allowing precise point-repair.
