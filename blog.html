The RICS Home Survey Standard

The RICS Home Survey Standard sets unified benchmarks across all licensed surveying practices in England and Wales. Key highlights include clear tripartite Condition Ratings (1, 2, 3), mandatory inspection protocols for roof voids, and direct guidance on buyer renegotiation.

EPC Standards & Period Properties

Retrofitting energy efficiency into historic properties requires careful building science. Applying impermeable insulation against solid brick walls can trap moisture inside masonry, causing concealed timber rot and spalling bricks. Our surveyors advise on breathable natural insulations (wood fibre and lime cork).

Building Safety Act Insights

The Building Safety Act 2022 fundamentally shifted cladding and non-cladding defect liabilities away from qualifying leaseholders. When purchasing an apartment in a building over 11 meters or 5 storeys, verifying Landlord Certificates and Deed of Certificate compliance is critical.
