Pre-Purchase Surveys (RICS Level 2 & 3).

Independent, comprehensive property condition assessments designed to protect your investment, uncover critical hidden defects, and give you confident price negotiation leverage.

Service Overview

Forensic Assessment Before You Commit.

Purchasing a residential property is typically the largest financial transaction of a lifetime. A standard mortgage valuation is conducted purely for the lender's security; it is neither a structural survey nor an inspection of building defects.

Hawk Property Surveys provides independent RICS Level 2 HomeBuyer Surveys and RICS Level 3 Full Structural Building Surveys. Our surveyors thoroughly examine the fabric of the property from the roof ridge to sub-floor voids, detailing immediate structural concerns, hidden dampness, rot, urgent repair liabilities, and anticipated long-term maintenance costs.

What the Survey Involves

  • Roof Structure & Coverings: Chimney stacks, lead valleys, parapet gutters, slate/tile condition, and roof timber truss inspection.
  • External Walls & Masonry: Brickwork spalling, mortar degradation, structural cracks, movement lintels, and damp-proof course integrity.
  • Sub-Floor & Timbers: Suspended floor ventilation, joist moisture content, dry rot, wet rot, and woodworm beetle activity.
  • Internal Ceilings, Walls & Joinery: Thermal bridging, damp mapping with radio-frequency meters, and plaster separation.
  • Drainage & Services Visual Check: Above-ground drainage, rainwater gullies, soil vent pipes, and incoming utility safety checks.

When is this Survey Required?

- Before Exchanging Contracts: Once an offer is accepted, to establish true repair liabilities before legal commitment.
- Level 2 Survey: Ideal for standard conventional houses, flats, and bungalows constructed of traditional materials after 1930.
- Level 3 Building Survey: Essential for Victorian, Edwardian, Georgian, or timber-framed properties, heavily altered or extended buildings, or properties planning major renovations.
