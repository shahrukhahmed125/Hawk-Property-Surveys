Cavity Wall & Tie Diagnostic Surveys.

Endoscopic optical inspection into the cavity void to assess wall tie corrosion, mortar droppings, insulation slumping, and outer leaf masonry stability.

Endoscopic Inspection

Look Inside Your Walls with Precision Borescopes.

In cavity wall construction, metal wall ties hold the external brick leaf and internal blockwork leaf together. In houses built between 1920 and 1980, mild steel or galvanized ties often corrode, expanding to cause horizontal stepped cracking or outer leaf detachment.

Hawk Property Surveys uses high-definition illuminated optical borescopes inserted through discrete 10mm mortar bed joints. We directly inspect tie density, rust degradation, retrofitted blown-fibre or bead insulation condition, and mortar snots bridging the cavity and causing damp penetration.

What We Detect

  • Wall Tie Corrosion & Delamination: Rust expansion causing horizontal joint cracking, outward masonry bowing, or insufficient tie density per square meter.
  • Cavity Bridging & Debris: Mortar droppings lodged across ties or accumulated at the cavity base above the DPC, transferring rainwater laterally.
  • Defective Cavity Insulation (CWI): Slumped, saturated, or patchy retrofitted insulation causing severe cold bridging and internal condensation patterns.
