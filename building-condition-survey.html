Building Condition Surveys.

Detailed assessment of the building envelope, internal fabric, structural components, and long-term maintenance requirements for residential owners, landlords, and commercial portfolios.

Service Overview

Holistic Building Health & Maintenance Planning.

A Building Condition Survey is an independent technical audit designed for current property owners, leaseholders, freeholders, and facilities managers who need an authoritative appraisal of their property's physical state.

Whether you are preparing a planned preventative maintenance (PPM) schedule, addressing persistent defects, or satisfying lease obligations, our chartered surveyors evaluate every accessible component. We distinguish cosmetic blemishes from underlying building pathology, prioritizing works by urgency.

What the Survey Involves

  • External Envelope: Cladding, masonry mortar condition, window frames, rendering, and rainwater disposal.
  • Roofing & Flashings: Pitch condition, flat roof membranes, parapets, coping stones, and chimney stacks.
  • Internal Finishes & Structure: Ceilings, partitions, load-bearing masonry, floor levelness, and structural cracking.
  • Services Age & Condition: Visual condition of electrical consumer units, plumbing manifolds, heating circulation, and ventilation.

When is this Survey Required?

- Planned Maintenance (PPM): Budgeting future capital expenditure across 1, 3, and 5-year cycles.
- Dilapidations & Lease Ends: Establishing tenant or landlord repair liabilities under commercial leases.
- Post-Disaster Review: Inspecting fabric following storm damage, flooding, or localized structural impact.
