Statutory Framework

Navigating Party Wall Matters Seamlessly.

If you or your neighbour are planning building works near a shared boundary, the Party Wall etc. Act 1996 provides a legal framework to prevent and resolve disputes. It grants building owners statutory rights to execute works while protecting adjoining neighbours from structural damage.

Hawk Property Surveys acts for Building Owners, Adjoining Owners, or as an impartial Agreed Surveyor across London and the Home Counties. Our members of the Faculty of Party Wall Surveyors ensure valid notice drafting, comprehensive pre-construction photographic condition schedules, and legally robust Party Wall Awards.

The Three Statutory Notice Categories

  • Section 1 (Line of Junction): Constructing a new wall at or astride the boundary line where no building previously stood (e.g., ground floor rear extensions).
  • Section 2 (Existing Party Structures): Cutting into a party wall for structural steel padstones (e.g. loft conversions), raising, rebuilding, or inserting a damp-proof course.
  • Section 6 (Adjacent Excavation): Excavating within 3 meters of a neighbour's foundation to a lower depth, or within 6 meters where a 45-degree angle of plane is breached.

A Schedule of Condition is a detailed written and photographic record of the adjoining property's current state immediately prior to the start of building work.
