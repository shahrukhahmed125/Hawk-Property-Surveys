Pathological Analysis

Protecting Structural Integrity.

Structural timbers in UK properties face two primary biological threats: wood-rotting fungal decay and wood-boring insect infestations. Left undetected, fungal attacks can compromise floor joists and roof trusses, creating severe safety hazards.

Hawk Property Surveys provides certified, independent timber investigations. We determine whether fungal decay is active or historic, identify the fungal species, and locate the vital moisture source that is keeping the decay alive.

Key Issues Identified

  • Dry Rot (Serpula lacrymans): The most malignant wood decay, capable of spreading through brickwork and masonry to destroy structural timbers in adjacent dry areas.
  • Wet Rot (Coniophora puteana & Fibroporia vaillantii): Frequent in unventilated suspended ground floors, window sills, and roof valleys subject to persistent water leaks.
  • Wood-Boring Beetles (Woodworm): Common Furniture Beetle (Anobium punctatum), Deathwatch Beetle (Xestobium rufovillosum), and House Longhorn Beetle inspection.
