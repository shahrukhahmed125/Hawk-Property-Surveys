Thermal Imaging Intelligence

Forensic Heat Loss & Moisture Profiling.

Every building surface emits infrared radiation based on its temperature and thermal emissivity. Infrared thermography translates this thermal radiation into visible, radiometric color maps.

Hawk Property Surveys utilizes calibrated FLIR thermal imaging cameras operated by certified thermographers. When combined with controlled internal heating, our thermal scans identify gaps in loft and cavity insulation, quantify heat escaping around window frames, highlight cold bridges where condensation occurs, and detect concealed moisture tracking behind plastered walls.

What Thermal Imaging Reveals

  • Insulation Continuity & Voids: Missing insulation batts in roofs, slumped cavity insulation, and poorly insulated dormer cheeks.
  • Thermal Bridging (Cold Spots): Steel lintels, concrete floor edges, and uninsulated junctions where condensation and toxic black mould repeatedly form.
  • Draught & Air Infiltration: Unsealed gaps around window frames, external door thresholds, and floor-to-wall junctions.
  • Underfloor Heating Mapping: Non-destructively tracing heating loops, verifying circulation balance, and locating pinhole leaks.
