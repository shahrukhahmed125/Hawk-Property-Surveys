2. Survey Process & Timelines

Depending on the property size, age, and survey discipline, an on-site inspection takes between 2 to 4.5 hours. For comprehensive Level 3 surveys on large Victorian properties, our surveyors take the necessary time to test every accessible element, inspect the roof void, and test moisture profiles.
Our written reports are typically dispatched within 3 to 5 working days following the inspection. In urgent legal exchange scenarios, an initial verbal summary can be provided by the inspecting surveyor within 24 hours of inspection.

3. Survey Types & Pathology

4. Pricing & Transparent Fees

All quotations provided by Hawk Property Surveys are 100% fixed upfront based on property size, location, and survey scope. We do not add surprise travel supplements, administrative surcharges, or post-survey consultation fees.
We accept bank transfer (BACS), debit cards, and major credit cards via our secure invoicing system.

5. Party Wall Matters (Act 1996)

You must serve notice if you intend to: build a new wall at or astride the boundary line (Section 1), cut into a shared party wall to insert steel beams or DPC (Section 2), or excavate foundations within 3 meters (or 6 meters for deep piles) of a neighbouring building to a lower depth (Section 6).
