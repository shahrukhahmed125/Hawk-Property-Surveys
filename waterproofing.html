Water Ingress Pathology

Independent Basement Protection Audits.

Basements, cellars, and retaining walls are subject to hydrostatic water pressure from surrounding earth and fluctuating water tables. Waterproofing failures can cause catastrophic structural flooding, mould contamination, and ruined fit-outs.

Hawk Property Surveys provides certified structural waterproofing surveys in accordance with BS 8102:2022 (Code of practice for protection of below ground structures against water from the ground). Because we do not install waterproofing systems or sell membranes, our assessments are strictly independent.

The Three Waterproofing Types We Inspect

  • Type A (Barrier Protection): External or internal bonded tanking membranes, multi-coat cementitious slurries, and asphalt barriers.
  • Type B (Structurally Integral Protection): Water-resisting reinforced concrete structures with waterstops at construction and day-work joints.
  • Type C (Drained Protection): Internal cavity drain stud membranes directing seepage behind walls into perimeter channels, flushed by dual sump pump stations with battery backup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions on Basement Waterproofing.

The most frequent causes of Type C failures are: lack of regular servicing for sump pumps, calcification/free-lime blockages in perimeter drainage channels, failure to install high-water level audible alarms, and lack of secondary battery backup during mains power cuts.
BS 8102 is the British Standard Code of Practice for the protection of below ground structures against water from the ground. It defines performance grades, water table assumptions, and requires risk mitigation strategies (including dual waterproofing protection where consequences of failure are severe).
