Non-Destructive Trace & Access

Precision Water Leak Detection Surveys.

Pinpoint concealed leaks in underfloor heating pipes, water mains, internal drainage, and flat roofs without unnecessary excavation or damaging decorative finishes.

Zero Destruction

Locate Hidden Water Leaks Fast.

A concealed water leak can silently cause thousands of pounds of structural timber rot, masonry saturation, and mould damage before becoming visible on surface finishes.

Hawk Property Surveys provides non-destructive acoustic and thermographic leak detection. Instead of indiscriminately ripping up hardwood flooring, digging concrete floor screeds, or knocking through tiled bathroom walls, our specialized instrumentation pinpoints the exact point of pipe failure to within a few centimeters.

Our Detection Technologies

  • Acoustic Listening Devices: High-sensitivity ground microphones and correlators that pick up the ultrasonic sound frequency of escaping pressurized water.
  • Tracer Gas Detection: Pressurizing pipework with safe, non-toxic hydrogen/nitrogen gas (5% H2 / 95% N2) that permeates concrete slabs and is detected by sniffing sensors.
  • High-Resolution Thermography: Capturing thermal anomalies from hot water central heating loops and cold water supply runs.
  • Insurance Trace & Access Documentation: Full photographic evidence compliant with UK building insurance policy requirements.
